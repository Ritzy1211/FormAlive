Privacy policy

FormAlive is a job-application assistant that runs locally in your browser. Your profile, résumé, and answers are encrypted on your device and never sent to us. This page explains exactly what that means.

Effective: May 22, 2026 · Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Summary 2. Who we are 3. Data we handle 4. Where it's stored 5. Optional AI features 6. Third parties 7. This website 8. Your rights 9. Children 10. Security 11. Changes 12. Contact

1. Summary (the honest one-pager)

  • FormAlive does not have a backend that stores your data. There is no FormAlive account.
  • Your profile, résumé, work history, snippets, and answers live in encrypted local storage inside the browser, protected by a passphrase only you know.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your application data to any third party.
  • The optional AI feature is off by default. If you turn it on, requests go directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, using your API key. We never see them.
  • This marketing website uses no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies. See the Cookies notice.

2. Who we are

In this policy, "FormAlive", "we", "us", and "our" refer to the makers of the FormAlive browser extension and this website at github.com/Ritzy1211/FormAlive. You can reach us at hi@formalive.app.

3. Data we handle

For transparency, here is every category of data the extension touches and where it lives.

Category Examples Location
Profile Name, email, phone, address, work eligibility, links Encrypted in your browser (chrome.storage.local)
Résumé The PDF you upload, extracted text, work history Encrypted in your browser
Snippets & overrides Saved answers, per-site corrections you've made Encrypted in your browser
Settings Locale, AI on/off, your AI provider & API key Encrypted in your browser
Per-site consent "Don't ask here" preferences Browser storage (not encrypted; no personal data)
Form context Labels & questions on the page being filled Processed in memory only; never persisted by us

We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, usage telemetry, crash reports, or product analytics.

4. Where your data is stored

All FormAlive data is stored inside your browser profile on the device you installed the extension on. The vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations). The passphrase never leaves your device, and we cannot recover it for you.

Uninstalling the extension removes the vault. Clearing your browser data for the extension does the same. There is no copy on our side because we have no server.

5. Optional AI features

FormAlive can optionally draft cover letters and long-form essay answers. This feature is off by default. When enabled:

  • You choose the provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) and supply your own API key.
  • Requests are sent directly from your browser to that provider over HTTPS. We are not in the path.
  • The request includes the question being answered and the minimum résumé/work-history context needed to produce a grounded answer.
  • Your provider's privacy policy applies to that traffic. Review it before enabling AI.
  • You can disable AI at any time from the extension's settings.

6. Third parties

The extension does not send your data to any third party except, if you turn it on, the AI provider you select (see §5). The job sites you choose to fill (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, etc.) receive exactly the information you submit through their forms — same as if you'd typed it. We are not affiliated with any of those services.

This marketing website loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS runtime from a public CDN. Those providers may receive standard request metadata (IP, user agent) inherent to loading any resource over the internet. We do not pass them any personal data and we do not place tracking cookies. See the Cookies notice.

7. This website

The site at formalive.app (and any GitHub Pages mirror) is a static marketing page. It does not require an account, does not run analytics, and does not place advertising cookies. The only client-side storage is a single first-party preference recording whether you've dismissed the cookie notice.

8. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device and not in any database we operate, your rights are exercised directly:

  • Access & portability: Export your vault from the extension's settings.
  • Correction: Edit any field in the extension at any time.
  • Erasure: Click "Reset vault" in the extension, or uninstall it.

If you live in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California, or another region with data-protection laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), the same applies: we don't act as a controller of any database of your data because no such database exists. If you have a specific question or a regulator request, write to hi@formalive.app.

9. Children

FormAlive is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly handle their data.

10. Security

We use modern, audited browser primitives (the Web Crypto API, AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA256) and ship the extension's source code publicly so anyone can verify the behavior described here. No system is perfectly secure — choose a strong passphrase, keep your device updated, and don't share your AI API keys.

11. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the extension handles data, we'll update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above. For small clarifications we'll just edit in place.

12. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email hi@formalive.app or open an issue on GitHub.
