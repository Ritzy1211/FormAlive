FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

Is FormAlive really free right now?
Yes. While we're in beta, every plan above runs at $0 and every feature is unlocked. You don't need a card and you don't need an account.
What happens when you exit beta?
Starter stays free, forever. Pro and Teams begin billing at the prices shown above. We'll give beta users at least 30 days notice and apply a launch discount that's locked in for as long as you stay subscribed.
Do I need to pay for the AI cover-letter feature today?
No. AI drafting is unlocked for everyone in beta. You only ever pay your AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) for tokens you use — FormAlive never proxies that traffic.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. When billing launches, you can cancel from your account page or by uninstalling the extension. There are no contracts for Starter or Pro; Teams plans can be monthly or annual.
Do you offer student or non-profit discounts?
Yes. Email us at hi@formalive.app with proof of status and we'll set you up with a discounted Pro seat. Job-seeker hardship discounts are also available — just ask.
Is there a refund policy?
When billing launches: 14-day no-questions refund for Pro, and pro-rata refunds for Teams within the first 30 days. We'd rather keep happy customers than unhappy ones.
What payment methods will you accept?
Major credit/debit cards via Stripe at launch, with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and SEPA/iDEAL where supported. Teams customers can request annual invoicing.
