FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where is my data stored?
Everything lives in chrome.storage.local on your computer, encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a key derived from your master passphrase via 600,000 iterations of PBKDF2-SHA256. We do not operate a server. There is no account to sign up for.
How does AI mode work? Do you see my résumé?
AI mode is opt-in and uses your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini. When you click "Fill", the extension calls those providers directly from your browser. We never proxy that traffic and we never receive your résumé, field labels, or any application content.
Can companies tell I'm using FormAlive?
No. To the website, filling looks identical to a human typing — FormAlive uses native input/change events and even respects React-controlled inputs. There is no fingerprint we leak.
What happens if I uninstall the extension?
Chrome wipes the encrypted vault when you remove the extension. There is nothing left on our end because nothing was ever sent to us. Clean slate, instantly.
Which sites does it support?
Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, BambooHR, Taleo / Oracle HCM, Workable, Indeed Apply, and LinkedIn Easy Apply have dedicated adapters. A generic rule engine handles thousands of other forms (Glassdoor, company career portals, internship sites, contact pages, etc.).
Is it open source?
Yes. The full source lives on GitHub. Audit it, fork it, send a PR.
